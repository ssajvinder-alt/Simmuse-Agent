A day of small jobs, handled.

Simmuse works from your messaging apps and on a schedule, so the small tasks stop eating your evening.

  • Replies to customersAnswers common questions in Telegram, WhatsApp and other chat apps you connect.
  • Keeps your listRemembers what you asked for across days, so you don't repeat yourself.
  • Runs on a scheduleA morning summary, a weekly report or a reminder, at the time you set.
  • Looks things upBrowses the web and reads files on your computer when a task needs them.
