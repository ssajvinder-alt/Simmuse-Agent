# Simmuse brand kit

**Name:** Simmuse (one word, capital S). Never "Hermes" or "Nous" in product UI.
**Attribution (required by the MIT license):** "Built on the open-source Hermes Agent by Nous Research."
Do not imply Nous endorses Simmuse.

**Line:** The assistant that runs your small business admin.
**Short:** Simmuse answers your customers, keeps your list and runs your routine jobs.

## Voice
Plain and specific. Say what it does, in the customer's words (messages, bookings, reports).
No hype numbers, no invented customers, no claims about earnings.
